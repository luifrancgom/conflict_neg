<h4>Purposes</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        <strong>The Nature of Negotiation:</strong> Understand the definition of negotiation,
        the elements in general of this process and the main types of negotiation that have been
        identified in the literature as well as the relationship between negotiation and conflict
        management.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Strategy and Tactics of Distributive Bargaining:</strong> Understand the basic
        elements of a distributive negotiation situation, as well as the strategies and tactics
        that can be applied in this context.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Strategy and Tactics of Integrative Negotiation:</strong> Understand the basic
        elements of an integrative negotiation situation, as well as the strategies and tactics
        that can be applied in this context.
    </li>
</ol>

<h4>Methodological Process</h4>

<ol>
    <li>Read and review Chapters 1, 2 and 3.</li>
    <li>
        Make a conceptual map where the elements mentioned in Chapters 1, 2 and 3
        are ordered in a logical manner so that they can be used as an input in the
        activity "Questionnaire 1" and "Midterm Exam Three Party Coalition Exercise Round 1".
    </li>
</ol>
<p>
    The realization of the conceptual map is not a qualifiable activity to be
    delivered, but a tool that can be used to understand the topics discussed in
    these parts of the e-book.
</p>
