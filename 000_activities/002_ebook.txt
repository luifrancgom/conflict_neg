<h4>Purposes</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        <strong>Negotiation - Strategy and Planning:</strong> Understand and explore the
        main elements of the negotiation strategy, the process to select a strategy as well
        as the way in which most negotiations evolve to effectively plan a negotiation.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Perception, Cognition, and Emotion:</strong> Understand how perceptions,
        knowledge and emotions affect a negotiation process.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Communication:</strong> Explore the tools and practices to improve
        communication processes in a negotiation.
    </li>
</ol>

<h4>Methodological Process</h4>

<ol>
    <li>Read and review Chapters 4, 6 and 7.</li>
    <li>
        Make a conceptual map where the elements mentioned in Chapters 4, 6 and 7
        are ordered in a logical manner so that they can be used as an input in the
        activity "Questionnaire 2" and "Midterm Exam Three Party Coalition Exercise Round 2".
    </li>
</ol>
<p>
    The realization of the conceptual map is not a qualifiable activity to be
    delivered, but a tool that can be used to understand the topics discussed in
    these parts of the e-book.
</p>
