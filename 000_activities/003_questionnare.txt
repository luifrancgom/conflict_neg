<h4>Structure and Format</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        The questionnaire consists of 6 questions and must be presented
        individually. Therefore, if it is answered in group, the grade will be zero
        (0).
    </li>
    <li>
        All 6 questions that are part of the questionnaire are of multiple choice
        with a unique correct answer and are presented in english.
    </li>
    <li>
        Each question has the same weight in the total score and will be scored on a
        scale of 50 points. In other words, each question has a maximum possible
        score of 8.33 points.
    </li>
</ol>

<h4>Available time</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        One (1) hour available to answer. Also, thirty (30) additional minutes are
        included in case there is a technical problem. In that sense in total, one
        and a half (1.5) hours are available.
    </li>
</ol>

<h4>Attemps allowed</h4>

<ol>
    <li>
        One and only one
    </li>
</ol>
